# CHNIP Indicator Reference Sheet

## 1. Purpose

This Indicator Reference Sheet provides the technical specifications for the indicators used to monitor the Community Health and Nutrition Improvement Project (CHNIP).

It ensures that indicators are consistently defined, measured, reported and interpreted throughout the project implementation period.

# 3. Indicator Technical Specifications

## CHN-OUT-001 — Children Screened for Malnutrition

**Indicator level:** Output

**Unit of measurement:** Number

**Definition:**
Number of children aged 0–59 months who were screened for malnutrition through project-supported screening activities during the reporting period.

**Baseline:** 0

**Annual target:** 1,200 children

**Data source:** Child screening register

**Means of verification:** Screening register and monthly activity report

**Collection frequency:** Monthly

**Disaggregation:**

* Sex
* Age group
* Community

**Responsible person:** Community Health Volunteer / M&E Officer

**Calculation:**
Count of eligible children screened during the reporting period.

**Interpretation:**
Higher achievement indicates greater coverage of planned screening activities, although data quality and duplication must be checked before interpreting performance.

---

## CHN-OUT-002 — Pregnant/Lactating Women Reached

**Indicator level:** Output

**Unit of measurement:** Number

**Definition:**
Number of pregnant and lactating women who received health and nutrition information through project-supported activities during the reporting period.

**Baseline:** 0

**Annual target:** 800 women

**Data source:** Outreach register

**Means of verification:** Outreach attendance register and activity report

**Collection frequency:** Monthly

**Disaggregation:**

* Beneficiary category
* Community

**Responsible person:** Community Health Volunteer / Program Officer

**Calculation:**
Count of eligible pregnant and lactating women reached during the reporting period.

**Interpretation:**
The indicator measures program reach and does not by itself demonstrate whether the information resulted in improved knowledge or behavior.

---

## CHN-OUT-003 — Health Education Sessions Conducted

**Indicator level:** Output

**Unit of measurement:** Number

**Definition:**
Number of structured community health and nutrition education sessions successfully completed during the reporting period.

**Baseline:** 0

**Annual target:** 60 sessions

**Data source:** Activity report

**Means of verification:** Session reports, attendance records and facilitator reports

**Collection frequency:** Monthly

**Disaggregation:**

* Community
* Topic
* Reporting period

**Responsible person:** Program Officer / M&E Officer

**Calculation:**
Count of completed and documented health education sessions.

**Interpretation:**
Only sessions supported by appropriate documentation should be counted as completed.

---

## CHN-OUT-004 — Referrals Completed

**Indicator level:** Output

**Unit of measurement:** Number

**Definition:**
Number of beneficiaries referred through project-supported activities who subsequently accessed the recommended health or nutrition service.

**Baseline:** 0

**Annual target:** 500 completed referrals

**Data source:** Referral register

**Means of verification:** Referral forms, facility records and referral follow-up records

**Collection frequency:** Monthly

**Disaggregation:**

* Sex
* Community
* Referral type

**Responsible person:** Case Worker / Community Health Volunteer

**Calculation:**
Count of referred beneficiaries with verified evidence of service utilization.

**Interpretation:**
A referral should not be considered completed solely because a referral was issued. Evidence that the beneficiary accessed the recommended service is required.

---

## CHN-OUT-005 — Community Health Volunteers Trained

**Indicator level:** Output

**Unit of measurement:** Number

**Definition:**
Number of community health volunteers who successfully completed the required project training during the implementation period.

**Baseline:** 0

**Annual target:** 100 volunteers

**Data source:** Training attendance records

**Means of verification:** Signed attendance sheets, training reports and completion records

**Collection frequency:** Quarterly

**Disaggregation:**

* Sex
* Community

**Responsible person:** Training Coordinator / M&E Officer

**Calculation:**
Count of volunteers meeting the project's training completion requirements.

**Interpretation:**
Attendance alone should not automatically be interpreted as successful training completion where assessment or completion criteria exist.

---

## CHN-OUT-006 — Beneficiaries Accessing Recommended Services

**Indicator level:** Outcome

**Unit of measurement:** Percentage

**Definition:**
Percentage of identified beneficiaries who accessed the health or nutrition service recommended to them during the reporting period.

**Baseline:** 0%

**Annual target:** 80%

**Data source:** Routine service records

**Means of verification:** Service registers, referral records and beneficiary follow-up records

**Collection frequency:** Quarterly

**Disaggregation:**

* Sex
* Community
* Service type

**Responsible person:** M&E Officer

### Calculation

**Percentage accessing recommended services =**

`Number of identified beneficiaries who accessed the recommended service ÷ Total number of identified beneficiaries × 100`

**Interpretation:**
A higher percentage indicates stronger uptake of recommended services. The result should be interpreted alongside referral completion, accessibility and data-quality findings.

# 4. Data Quality Requirements

All indicators should meet the following minimum data-quality dimensions:

### Completeness

Required reporting fields should be populated and submitted for the reporting period.

### Accuracy

Reported values should correspond with available source documents.

### Timeliness

Reports should be submitted within the agreed reporting deadline.

### Consistency

The same indicator should produce consistent results across relevant reporting tools and periods.

### Validity

Data should conform to the approved indicator definition, eligibility criteria and reporting period.

# 6. Indicator Performance Classification

For numerical output indicators:

**Achievement (%) = Actual ÷ Target × 100**

Performance categories:

| Achievement | Status          | Interpretation                                                 |
| ----------: | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
|        ≥90% | On Track        | Performance is close to or above the expected target           |
|      70–89% | Needs Attention | Performance requires monitoring and possible corrective action |
|        <70% | Off Track       | Significant performance gap requiring investigation            |

For percentage indicators, performance should be assessed against the approved target and appropriate denominator rather than applying the same logic blindly.

---

## 7. Important MEAL Note

Indicator performance should not be interpreted independently of data quality.

A low reported achievement may indicate genuine program underperformance, but it may also result from:

* Incomplete reporting
* Late submissions
* Missing source documents
* Incorrect calculations
* Duplicate or omitted records
* Inconsistent indicator definitions
* Data-entry errors

Therefore, data-quality assessment should precede major programmatic conclusions.
